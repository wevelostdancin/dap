world-110m.json
===============

Country outlines derived from Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 Countries:

  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
  https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector
  geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson

Natural Earth data is in the public domain: "no permission is needed to use Natural
Earth data; you may use it for any purpose, including commercial" (CC0 1.0 public
domain dedication).

The bundled file is a compact derivative, not the original GeoJSON: only polygon
rings are kept, coordinates are rounded to two decimals, consecutive duplicate
points and rings with fewer than four points are dropped, and all attributes are
removed. Format:

  {"source": "<attribution>", "rings": [[[lon, lat], ...], ...]}

Coordinates are WGS84 degrees. The chart renderer projects them equirectangularly.
